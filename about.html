ABOUT TRAVELMATE

Travel is personal.
So should be the way you choose it.

TravelMate started with a simple thought: choosing a destination shouldn't feel like scrolling through a thousand identical lists.

01 — WHY WE EXIST

Less “top 10 places”.
More “this feels like me”.

Some people want a crowded beach and loud music. Some want an old street, a quiet temple and a cup of tea. Neither is wrong.

TravelMate is a small recommendation website built around that idea. Instead of pretending there is one perfect trip for everyone, we make it easier to start with what you actually enjoy.

01

Curious

We like places with a story, a view, a smell, or something you haven't seen before.

02

Simple

Travel planning is already enough work. Finding inspiration shouldn't be.

03

Human

A destination can look perfect online and still not be your thing. That's completely okay.
